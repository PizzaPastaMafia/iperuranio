Raster Organ

An image synth built to SPEC.md rev 2. Each row is a sine voice on the selected map, anchored at the bottom row. The red plane plays left and the green plane plays right as two separate mono images with their own notes. Yellow plays both, and blue is silent.

Ready
Paint on the canvas. Click the strip on the left to hear one row. Shaded rows are at or above 20 kHz and stay silent.

Brush

Aspect, angle and hardness shape Round, Spray and Erase. Note always draws a one-pixel-wide line. Spacing works with every tool.

WaveSynth

Sine is the measured wave. Tri, saw and square are unmeasured extras, band-limited below 20 kHz.

Amplitude map

Gain by row height, bottom on the left and top on the right, stretched to Size y. The default line rises 28% bottom to top. Drag to draw.

Picture

Space play/stop · q cycle channel · j swap left/right · Ctrl/Cmd Z undo. Export WAV saves the same render as a 32-bit float stereo file. Renders at 44.1 kHz and at MetaSynth's raw level, with no normalization; Out adds playback gain only. Tempo moves in whole samples per pixel at 32 pixels per beat. Each row and channel keeps one oscillator whose phase advances only while that row is lit, so a new note picks up where the last one stopped. Changing Size y keeps the bottom rows and adds or removes rows at the top. Row numbers in the readout count from the top, as in SPEC.md.
